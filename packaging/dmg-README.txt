Kaleido Serial Bridge

Install:    drag "Kaleido Serial Bridge" onto "Applications", then open it from Applications.
            It lives in the menu bar and opens a setup guide the first time.

Uninstall:  quit the app from its menu, move it from Applications to the Trash, and delete the
            folder .kaleido-bridge in your home folder (Finder: Go > Go to Folder... > ~/.kaleido-bridge).
            If you had turned on "Open the app at login", turn it off first, or remove the app in
            System Settings > General > Login Items.

Tested with the roaster idle and the heater off. Stay with the roaster while it is hot.
More: README.md of the project.
